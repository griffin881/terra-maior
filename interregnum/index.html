Falsifier board

The auto signal is computed daily from the data where a falsifier is measurable. The verdict is the latest human judgement from the monthly check. When they disagree, the disagreement is the thing to look at.

Indicators

Latest readings, then the series behind them. Dashed lines are falsifier thresholds; the shaded band is P2's predicted range.

Hand-tracked series

No free machine-readable source. Add rows in data/manual.json; a series charts once it has two points.

Prediction markets

Polymarket "Yes" price, daily. Crowd odds are an input to check your reasoning against, not a verdict. Markets are configured in predictions.json.

Event feed

Curated events (hand-entered in data/events.json) plus a news feed pulled from GDELT and tagged by keyword. Treat the auto tags as a sorting aid: check the source before counting anything as evidence.

    Loop map

    A qualitative causal-loop sketch. Arrow signs give polarity: + means the two move together, − means they move oppositely. An even number of − links around a loop makes it reinforcing (R); an odd number makes it balancing (B). ‖ marks a delay, which is why the system can't snap back fast.

    Monthly falsification checks

    For each prediction: holding, wobbling, or broken. Update the predictions; don't defend them. New entries go in data/checks.json. A draft with the month's auto signals is generated in checks/ on the 1st.

    Ledger: what this framework hides

    • Cybernetics makes the transition look automatic. The loops describe pressures, not destinies; people misjudge, panic, or get lucky.
    • Dialectics makes the outcome look progressive. The interregnum can produce something worse than the hegemon. Negation is not redemption.
    • Value theory tempts economism. States have irreducible logics (prestige, regime survival, security dilemmas).
    • The prediction game flatters the predictor. Hold these like a short position: defined, falsifiable, closable.
