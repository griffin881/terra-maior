    The Qur’an names each of these gardens. Ordering them as eight ranks follows a popular tradition, and scholars differ on the order. What is authentically reported is that Jannah has a hundred levels and that al-Firdaws is the highest (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2790).
